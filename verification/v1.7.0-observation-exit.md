# Bounded observation during process exit

Large compiler images can exit successfully while releasing a multi-gigabyte
heap. Linux can stop reporting VmRSS before wait4 can reap the process. A
100 ms grace classified a successful whole-compiler generation as a harness
failure during that interval.

The observer now allows one second for this exit transition, still bounded by
the original wall deadline. It records that grace in every observation. Missing
RSS beyond the grace remains a harness failure and the owned process is killed
and reaped. The final wait4 peak is checked against the RSS cutoff even when no
usable sample was available before exit. Earlier cutoff reasons are preserved.

`python3 tests/mgnc/v150-measure-harness.py` checks delayed exit with missing
RSS, missing RSS through a wall deadline, persistent unavailability and a
final peak above the limit. Existing stream, exit-code, real RSS and timeout
checks remain. `python3 tests/mgnc/v160-measure-harness.py` also checks process
group cleanup and surviving descendants.

The compiler, runtime, qualified seed and historical observations are unchanged.
The prior failed observation remains failed; fresh generation and performance
runs are required. This changes no formal wall-time or RSS acceptance budget.
