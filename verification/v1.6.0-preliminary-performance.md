# Complete-backend preliminary performance

All 103 frozen cells completed one warm-up and three fresh recorded processes serially.
Every run met its expected result, stream, artifact and unchanged-input checks.
[Measurements and identities](v1.6.0-preliminary-performance.json) preserve every sample;
original raw results and streams are retained separately with digests.

Whole C0 check/lower medians were 61.982/88.710 seconds. Whole C1 check/lower
medians were 183.101/313.405 seconds. Maximum 64-KiB List lower/build medians
were 166.742/315.911 seconds, with peak RSS 1,224.23/1,297.07 MiB.
The maximum List build warm-up was faster than its recorded samples; it was
not substituted for them. Medium-to-maximum List compiler growth is nonlinear.
No general linear compiler-complexity claim is made from these finite cells.

The same compiler also has fifteen checkpoint-based lifetime observations,
including bounded, retained and repeated-I/O success/decode/read-error series.
Those observations are preliminary evidence for choosing reclamation criteria;
they are not continuous heap-peak measurements or fresh formal acceptance.

The [adopted budget](v1.6.0-performance-budget.md) fixes purpose-specific limits
before new formal measurement. Preliminary success is not formal performance,
whole-version implementation acceptance, or a release.
